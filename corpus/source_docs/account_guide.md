# Account Guide

## Overview

This guide explains the basic account-management procedures for customers.

## Creating an Account

Customers can create an account by providing the required registration information through the account registration flow.

Required information:
- Name
- Email address
- Password

After registration, customers can sign in using their email address and password.

## Signing In

To sign in:
1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Enter the email address associated with the account.
3. Enter the account password.
4. Submit the sign-in form.

## Password Reset

If a customer cannot remember their password:
1. Open the password-reset flow.
2. Enter the email address associated with the account.
3. Follow the password-reset instructions sent to that email address.
4. Create a new password.

## Account Security

Customers should:
- Keep their password private.
- Use a strong, unique password.
- Avoid sharing account credentials.
- Contact support if they suspect unauthorized access.

## Updating Account Information

Customers can update available account information from their account settings.

Typical information may include:
- Name
- Email address
- Contact information

## Support

If a customer cannot access their account or cannot complete a password reset, they should contact customer support.
