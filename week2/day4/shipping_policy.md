# Shipping Policy

## Overview

This policy describes standard shipping and delivery procedures for customer orders.

## Processing Orders

Orders are processed after they are successfully placed and payment has been confirmed.

Processing time is separate from transit time.

## Delivery Time

Standard delivery typically takes **3–5 business days** after an order has been processed.

Actual delivery time may vary depending on:
- Destination
- Carrier conditions
- Weather
- Holidays
- Other circumstances affecting transportation

## Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address.

An incorrect or incomplete address may cause a delivery delay or failed delivery.

## Tracking

When tracking information is available, customers can use the tracking information associated with their order to monitor delivery progress.

## Delayed Deliveries

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery window, the customer should first check the available tracking information.

If the shipment remains delayed, the customer should contact support with their order details.

## Damaged Packages

Customers should inspect packages when they arrive.

If a package or product is damaged during delivery, the customer should contact support and provide:
- Order details
- A description of the damage
- Photos of the damaged package or product when available

## Delivery Exceptions

Delivery may take longer than the standard estimate because of circumstances outside the company's control, including severe weather, carrier disruptions, public holidays, or incorrect delivery information.

## Support

For shipping questions or delivery problems, customers should contact customer support and provide their order details.
